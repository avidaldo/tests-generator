# Register and Tone Rules

How questions and options should read. Part of the [Question Rules](../questions_rules.md) module set.

## 1. Clarity over ornate formality

Conceptual difficulty is welcome; needless complexity is not. Prefer clear, direct wording over a loaded, over-formal, or convoluted style. A hard question should be hard because of the concept, not because of the prose.

## 2. No "trust that…" framing

Never use expressions like "y confiar en que…" / "and trust that…". In technical contexts things work in a defined way; they do not rely on hoping an outcome turns out as expected.

## 3. No loaded negative adjectives as tells

Avoid adjectives that openly signal "this is the wrong one", such as "enorme", "confuso", "caótico". They leak the answer and cheapen the distractor.

## 4. No childish or strawman distractors

Distractors must be credible technical mistakes, not jokes or strawmen.

- ❌ `VS Code no puede ser un IDE real porque no es un "verdadero IDE" como PyCharm.`

## 5. Consistent register across options

The correct answer and all distractors should share tone, length range, and specificity, so register itself never distinguishes them (reinforces [anti-bias.md](anti-bias.md)).
